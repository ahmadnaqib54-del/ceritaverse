THE HOUSE WITHOUT A DOOR · CHAPTER 1

The Knock at 2:17

Jack had lived in the house for eleven years. He knew every crack in the ceiling, every loose floorboard and every sound the old pipes made when the temperature dropped.

That was why the knocking frightened him.

Two knocks. A pause. Then one more.

Jack looked at the clock.

2:17 AM.

He walked into the hallway and stopped. At the end of it was a wall. There had always been a wall there.

But tonight, there was a door.

He reached for the handle. From the other side, someone whispered his name.
